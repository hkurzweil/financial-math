What does "median salary" mean? The median salary is the middle value — half of people in that job earn more, and half earn less. It's a realistic starting point. Look up the median for your specific career in the city you want to live in, not just a national average. Salaries in rural Colorado are typically lower than coastal cities.

Understanding your taxes

Gross pay ≠ take-home pay Every paycheck has money automatically withheld for taxes before you ever see it. In the U.S. you pay federal income tax (based on progressive brackets — higher earners pay a higher rate), Colorado state income tax (a simple flat 4.4%), and FICA, which funds Social Security and Medicare. The 50 / 30 / 20 guideline A popular starting framework: spend roughly 50% of take-home pay on needs (rent, food, utilities), 30% on wants (dining out, hobbies, subscriptions), and save at least 20%. This isn't a law — use it as a compass, not a cage. The ranges shown are realistic for southwestern Colorado.
