Why 20%? Putting 20% down means you avoid Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI) — an extra monthly fee (usually $50–200/month) that the lender charges when you borrow more than 80% of the home's value. It also means you start with equity in your home from day one and your monthly payments are lower.

Your mortgage

How a mortgage works A mortgage is a loan from a bank to buy a home. You pay it back in equal monthly payments over many years. Each payment covers two things: interest (the bank's fee for lending you money) and principal (actual paydown of the loan). Early in the loan, most of your payment is interest — this is called amortization.

Why interest adds up so much With a 30-year mortgage, the bank earns interest on a large balance for a long time. In the early years, almost all of your payment is interest — you're barely touching the principal. This is why paying even a little extra each month early on can save you tens of thousands of dollars in the long run.

Stage 2 — Buying a Home

Complete all four steps below. Your Google Doc should already be open from Stage 1.

✏️ Step 1 — In your Google Doc
Switch to your Google Doc. Scroll to the bottom of what you wrote in Stage 1. Press Enter twice and type this bold heading: Section 4: Buying My First Home. Press Enter and write a short paragraph (3–4 sentences) that describes the home you found on Zillow:
  • What city is it in? What type of home is it — house, condo, townhome?
  • How many bedrooms/bathrooms? Any notable features?
  • Why did you choose this specific home?
  • What is the listing price?
📸 Step 2 — Take a screenshot
On this page, take a screenshot of the two stat cards showing the down payment (20%) and the loan amount — the gray boxes right below the home price input in Section 1. Then take a second screenshot of the savings timeline block — the large green-colored box that shows how long it will take you to save the down payment.
📋 Paste into your doc
Switch to your Google Doc. After your paragraph about the home, press Enter twice and paste both screenshots one after the other (paste the down payment/loan screenshot first, then the timeline screenshot below it).
✏️ Write below the screenshots
Write a paragraph (4–6 sentences) analyzing your down payment and savings timeline:
  • How large is your down payment? Does that number feel like a lot or a little to you?
  • How many months or years will it take you to save that amount based on your Stage 1 savings rate? Does that timeline feel realistic?
  • What would you have to change to get there faster — save more, earn more, or choose a less expensive home?
  • At what approximate age would you be able to buy this home?
📸 Step 3 — Take a screenshot
In Section 3 (Your Mortgage), take a screenshot of the grand total cost breakdown table — the box that shows the home purchase price, your down payment, total payments to the bank, total interest paid, and the grand total you'll spend. This is the "true cost of this loan" section.
📋 Paste into your doc
Switch to your Google Doc. Press Enter twice below your last paragraph, type the bold heading Section 5: The Real Cost of a Mortgage, press Enter, and paste the screenshot.
✏️ Write below the screenshot
Write a paragraph (5–7 sentences) about the true cost of the mortgage. This is often the most eye-opening part of the whole project — be honest about your reaction:
  • What is the total amount you will pay the bank over the full life of the loan?
  • How much of that is interest — money you pay the bank just for the privilege of borrowing — versus money that actually goes toward owning the home?
  • How does the total cost compare to the original Zillow listing price? For example, if the home listed for $300,000 and you will pay $550,000 total, that's nearly twice the asking price.
  • Does knowing this change how you think about buying a home versus renting? Why or why not?
  • What would happen to your total interest if you chose a 15-year loan instead of a 30-year loan? (You do not need to calculate this — just think about it and share your reasoning.)
📸 Step 4 — Take a screenshot
In Section 4 (True Monthly Cost of Owning), take a screenshot of the full ownership cost table — from "Mortgage payment" down to "Total monthly housing cost."
📋 Paste into your doc
Switch to your Google Doc. Press Enter twice below your last paragraph and paste the screenshot. No new heading needed — this is part of the same section.
✏️ Write below the screenshot
Write a short paragraph (3–5 sentences):
  • What is your total monthly housing cost as a homeowner (including mortgage, taxes, insurance, and maintenance)?
  • How does that compare to the rent you had in Stage 1? Is homeownership more or less expensive per month than renting?
  • Were you surprised by any of the additional costs beyond the mortgage — like property taxes or the maintenance fund?
  • Even if monthly ownership costs more than renting, what are reasons someone might still prefer to own?
